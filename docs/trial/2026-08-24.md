The MD approved a **Change waiting on the Project Director**. Status went
`pending → approved` with no refusal.

This is `FIX-THIS.md` B4 (`roles.ts:119`, `if (role === "owner") return true`)
happening in operation, not just in code. On a real desk this is the control that
stops one person originating and approving the same item.

**Needs a product decision:** either the MD is scoped like every other seat, or
the four-eyes claim is retired from the spec.
